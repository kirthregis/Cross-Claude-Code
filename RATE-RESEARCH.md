# Dubai DJ rate research — where DJ Emy sits

Market research conducted August 2026 to replace guessed rates with defensible
numbers. Sources are public rate cards, agency pricing pages, salary aggregators
and practitioner forums — cited inline.

---

## 1. The market, tier by tier

Multiple independent Dubai sources converge on the same four-tier structure:

| Tier | Per event (AED) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Entry-level / beginner | 1,500 – 3,000 | Bella Entertainment; Box Entertainment |
| Mid-tier professional | 3,000 – 5,500 | Bella Entertainment |
| Professional event DJ | 3,500 – 8,000 | Box Entertainment |
| Premium / top-tier | 6,000 – 15,000+ | Bella Entertainment |
| Celebrity / international | 20,000+ | Box Entertainment |

Cross-checks:
- **Club nights:** AED 1,000–5,000/night, "established names earning the higher
  end" (djsdubai.com).
- **Corporate / weddings:** AED 2,500–10,000 depending on scale (djsdubai.com);
  premium wedding DJ + MC 7,000–15,000+ (Box).
- **Marketplace averages:** GigHeaven lists 664 Dubai club DJs averaging
  AED 2,356, range 179–25,924; 790 event DJs averaging AED 2,565, range
  176–53,837. These averages are dragged down by hobbyists — the *ceiling*
  is the useful signal.
- **Residencies:** AED 3,000/month (small bars) up to 20,000–30,000+/month for
  major venue groups (Soundtribe). A club-run residency is commonly ~15,000/month
  all-in with visa and DJ licence covered (practitioner, r/DJs).

**Important structural note:** the low marketplace averages (~AED 2,400) reflect
a saturated pool of part-time and open-format DJs. Soundtribe notes explicitly
that high-supply trending genres suppress rates. Emy is not in that pool.

## 2. Why Emy prices above the median

Her EPK establishes five things that move her out of the commodity tier:

1. **FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022 + FIFA Arab Cup 2025 — official tournament DJ.**
   This is the single strongest lever. It is a credential almost no Dubai
   working DJ has, and it maps directly onto the "celebrity or international"
   band for brand and festival buyers who price on credibility, not hours.
2. **Genre scarcity.** One of few female Afro House / Afro Tech DJs holding a
   peak-time floor in the GCC. Scarcity is the opposite of the oversupplied
   open-format pool that sets the AED 2,400 average.
3. **Bilingual EN/AR floors.** Directly monetisable for Eid, National Day,
   Khaleeji and mixed-market corporate work — a real constraint for most bookers.
4. **Full agency representation (EVG).** Contracted, accountable, one point of
   contact. Agency-repped artists price above direct-booking DJs — several Dubai
   DJs market "no agency fees" as their *discount* pitch, which confirms the spread.
5. **Named venue history.** Zeus Jumeirah Beach (Eid headline, peak-time),
   Amwaj Rooftop residency-style sessions, international touring (Trinidad 2025).

**Position: top of "premium", entering "celebrity/international" for brand work.**

### Residency guidance (not yet automated)
Market: 15,000–30,000+/month for established DJs at major venue groups.

## 4. Negotiating context

- **Peak season is Oct–Apr.** Book-early pressure is real; the engine already
  applies +15% to +35% across Oct–Jan.
- **Summer (Jun–Sep) is soft** — the 0.80 multiplier is correct. Consider
  residencies and corporate work to fill it, since corporate budgets are less
  seasonal.
- **"That's above our budget"** — the AED 2,400 marketplace average is what
  bookers will quote at her. The counter is that the average is a pool of
  part-time open-format DJs; the comparable set is FIFA-credentialled,
  agency-repped, genre-scarce artists.
- **VAT:** UAE VAT is 5%. Rates here are pre-VAT; the contract states fees are
  net of VAT where applicable.

## 5. Confidence and caveats

⚠️ **These remain market-derived estimates, not Emy's realised fees.** They are
defensible in a negotiation but they are not history. The single highest-value
improvement to the pricing engine is **5–10 of her actual past bookings**
(venue, date, fee) entered at `/profile`. Real history beats the best research.
