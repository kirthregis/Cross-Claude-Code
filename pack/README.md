## The 30-second version

**Someone asks about a booking:**
1. Look up the venue type in **1-RATE-CARD** → get the *Ask*.
2. Apply adjustments (Friday? +25%. Two days away? +25%. Want exclusivity? +30%).
3. Copy the matching pitch from **2-PITCH-SCRIPTS**, paste in the number, send.

**They push back on price:** open **3-NEGOTIATION**, find the objection, read the reply.

**They say yes:** send **4-CONTRACT-TEMPLATE** with the blanks filled. Get the
50% deposit *before* holding the date.

---

## Three rules that protect the money

1. **Never quote below the Floor.** It's the walk-away, not an opening position.
2. **A deposit is the only confirmation.** A verbal "we'll confirm later" is not
   a booking, and it blocks her from taking paid work that night.
3. **Exclusivity is a product.** A radius clause stops her working elsewhere
   that week — it costs +30%, never granted free.

## Before sending the contract to a real client

Two blanks remain in **4-CONTRACT-TEMPLATE** and **5-INVOICE-TEMPLATE**:

- `PLACEHOLDER — EVG registered legal entity name & trade licence no.`
- Bank details on the invoice

Both are on the EVG business licence and the Mashreq statement. Fill them in
once, and every future document is complete.

⚖️ The contract is a solid industry-standard agreement covering fees,
cancellation, technical requirements, IP, press rights and Dubai law — but have
a UAE lawyer read it once before using it on a high-value booking.
