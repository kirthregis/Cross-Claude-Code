# Get her music out there — free

The top places DJs and producers push music, checked for 2026. Everything the
studio exports is already in the exact format these sites want — **48 kHz WAV
master, -14 LUFS, 3000×3000 cover, title/description/tags** — so she can post
anywhere without reformatting.

The same list lives **in the app** at **`/studio/distribute`** (one-tap links).

---

## 1) Get on Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok — for free

To land on the big streaming stores you go through a distributor (Spotify
doesn't accept direct uploads). The genuinely free options in 2026:

| Service | Free deal | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **RouteNote** | Free, you keep 85% of royalties | Reliable, delivers to Spotify/Apple/TikTok/YouTube Music + 20 stores. Paid tier = 100%. Processing ~2–4 weeks. |
| **FreshTunes** | Near-100% royalties | Fastest — releases live in ~48 hours. Good for quick drops. |
| **ONErpm** | Free with a commission | Solid all-rounder, marketing support. |
| **UnitedMasters** | Free DEBUT tier, you keep 90% | 50+ stores + brand-deal opportunities. |
| **SoundOn** | Free, 100% year one | ByteDance's distributor — deepest TikTok integration. |

> ⚠️ Distributors change their free tiers (Amuse and TuneCore dropped theirs).
> Verify the current terms on the site before committing — and never pay for
> "guaranteed streams" or "playlist placement"; that's how scams work.

> ⚠️ **AI-disclosure note:** several distributors ask whether the track is
> AI-generated when you upload. Her music is her own work — answer honestly.

## 2) Host her DJ mixes (free)

| Service | Free deal | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **YouTube** | Free, unlimited | Her main home. Use the Release tab handoff pack. |
| **SoundCloud** | Free tier (3h of uploads) | Biggest DJ community. Next Pro ($) removes limits. |
| **Mixcloud** | Free (10 uploads, rotate) | Licensed for DJ sets — no copyright takedowns on mixes. |
| **HearThis.at** | 100 free uploads + free live | DJ-friendly, small but loyal crowd. |
| **1001Tracklists** | Free | The DJ mix database — post the set's tracklist. This is how sets get found and how she builds DJ credibility. |

> Copyright note: SoundCloud is stricter on mixes that sample commercial
> tracks; Mixcloud is the safe home for those, and YouTube generally works
> with claims (ad-sharing) rather than takedowns for her style of set.

## 3) Submit to record labels (free)

- **LabelRadar** (https://labelradar.com) — the free, legitimate route into
  electronic labels. Submit tracks to Afro House / Afro Tech / House labels
  with real feedback. No fees, no scams.

## 4) Promote

- **Instagram / TikTok** — cut a Reel with the cover art + mastered audio
  (the Artwork tab's 3000×3000 JPEG and the Master tab's WAV are exactly what
  you need), link to the YouTube mix.
- Consistent posting on YouTube is her proven channel (her Afro House Mix
  2026 does exactly this) — the studio makes the format perfect every time,
  so the only job left is hitting Upload.

## Suggested release ritual

1. Master → export 24-bit WAV + cover from the Release tab.
2. YouTube (handoff pack) + Mixcloud/SoundCloud for the mix; tracklist on
   1001Tracklists.
3. Track version → RouteNote or FreshTunes to the stores, and LabelRadar to
   labels.
4. Reels on Instagram/TikTok pointing at the YouTube mix.
