# Press assets

Drop DJ Emy's approved press images here. They are referenced by the press pack
generator (`src/lib/contract.ts`) and served at `/press/<filename>`.

Recommended set:
- `portrait-black.jpg`  — hero portrait, portrait orientation
- `portrait-landscape.jpg` — landscape crop for banners/artwork
- `logo.png` — transparent PNG
- `bio.md` — 50 and 150 word versions

Venues receive a limited licence to these for the specific engagement only
(see Clause 7 of the generated contract).
